## B2

**The benchmark orders the settings and overstates the rate.** Measured bearing error against the resource product R = N_s N_c m(m^2-1), fitted with RMSE^2 = a R^-b + c so that the irreducible floor cannot be absorbed into the exponent. The fitted b = 0.403, 95% interval [0.295, 0.548], excludes the b = 1 the Cramer-Rao bound predicts, and the floor is 12.53 degrees against a no-information reference of 16.833. The interval is the joint-seed median bootstrap from corrected_statistics.json, not a bootstrap computed here: an earlier version resampled the MEAN while the point estimate used the MEDIAN, which gave an interval narrower than the estimator supports. The reduced-aperture point lies below the fit because the aperture factor inflates R without buying accuracy on these recordings, which is why the fit holds m = 4 fixed; it carries the same seed spread as every other point, so the departure can be judged. The m = 1 setting has m(m^2-1) = 0, so R = 0 and it has no position on a logarithmic axis at all; it is annotated rather than dropped, because reaching 13.92 degrees with no angular information whatever is the sharpest evidence that the aperture term is inert here. Error bars are the seed standard deviation.

## B3

## B6
